Most prep stops at the theory round

      
Question banks and playlists get you through screening. Then someone slides a packet capture across the table, or asks how you would contain a compromised host at 2am, and there is no playlist for that. That is the round SecMox is built for.


      

        What candidates practice
        What interviewers actually ask
      

      
Define the CIA triad
Here is an alert queue. What do you pick up first, and why that one?

      
List the OWASP Top 10
This login endpoint is vulnerable. Show me on the screen.

      
Explain the TCP handshake
Read this capture. What is this host doing, and for how long?

      
Name the phases of incident response
Your EDR flagged a signed binary. Walk me through the next hour.

      
Say you are a fast learner
Tell me about a time you were wrong about a finding.

    

  


  

    

      
Four things you practice here

      
Each one targets a round that candidates lose. None of them is a video you watch.


      


        

          

            

            

            

          

          
Question bank

          
With the follow-up attached

          
Every question carries the question that comes after it, because that is where candidates lose the room. Filter by role: SOC, pentest, GRC, cloud, network.

        


        

          

            

            

          

          
Browser labs

          
Real artifacts, no VM setup

          
Packet captures, auth logs, memory strings, alert queues, phishing headers. They open in the browser and you answer what you see. Nothing to install.

        


        

          

            

            

          

          
Mock interviews

          
Timed, graded, on the record

          
Pick a role and a company pattern. Get grilled for 30 minutes, then get a transcript that marks where your answer went thin and what the interviewer was fishing for.

        


        

          

            

            

          

          
Scenario writing

          
Where judgment shows up

          
Write out how you would handle an incident end to end. Scored against the same rubric a hiring panel uses, so you find out whether your reasoning holds before it costs you an offer.
