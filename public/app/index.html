Mock rounds

The two things we are building next. Neither is live yet, so nothing here runs. We would rather show you the plan than hide an empty button.

Coming soon

AI mock interview

Pick a role and a company pattern, then get grilled for thirty minutes by an interviewer that follows your answers instead of reading a script. At the end, a transcript marking where you went thin and what each question was really testing.

Why not yet: it runs on a model for every session, so we are getting the grilling sharp and the cost sane before it goes live.

Coming soon

Live mock round

Forty-five minutes, one-on-one, with someone who has actually sat on the hiring side of the table. Run on a real company's pattern, with a written report in your inbox the next day.

Why not yet: a live round is only as good as the interviewer behind it, so this opens when we can staff it properly, not before.

Until then, the follow-up ladder in Practice is the closest thing: it keeps asking the next question the way a real round would.
